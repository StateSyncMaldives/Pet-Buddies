# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Adopters / viewers** in Greater Malé (Maldives) browsing adoptable cats and legally-importable birds — casual, mobile-first, often coming from Facebook rehoming groups; no account needed to browse, save, or report.
- **Listing owners** — individuals and verified organizations (rescues, NGOs, community groups) publishing adoption listings; listing creation requires sign-in.
- **Moderators** — reviewing pending listings in the review queue and recording moderation events.
- **Lost/found reporters** — people reporting a missing or found animal, routed to an organization by species.

## Product Purpose

Replace informal Facebook-group pet rehoming with one trustworthy place: verified rescue organizations, reviewed individual listings, saved listings, adoption inquiries, lost/found report routing, and a seeded vet/clinic directory. Success = adoptions and reports flow through reviewed, accountable listings instead of unmoderated posts. Dogs are out of scope (import prohibited in the Maldives); bird listings are restricted to a legal species allowlist.

## Brand Personality

Soft, friendly, trustworthy. The identity is a sitting cat with birds cradled in a heart, in a soft pastel palette (powder blue + blush pink), with the Quicksand wordmark. The app UI itself is crisp and product-like — cool neutral greys, calm surfaces — so the pastel brand moments (logo, hero, verified badge, status chips) stand out warmly against a quiet shell.

## Anti-references

- Facebook Marketplace / classifieds-style listing grids — the product exists to NOT feel like an ad board (domain glossary bans "post", "ad", "seller").
- Petfinder-style dense corporate directories — this is community-scale, warm, curated.
- Generic SaaS dashboard styling for the consumer surfaces (the review queue may be utilitarian; Browse must not be).

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the product** — verification badges, moderation states, and receipts are first-class UI, never buried.
2. **The pet is the hero** — photography and names lead; chrome recedes. Listing UI serves the animal, not the interface.
3. **Warm brand, quiet shell** — pastel brand color appears at moments (hero, badges, primary actions); the shell stays cool-neutral and calm.
4. **One vocabulary everywhere** — the domain glossary (CONTEXT.md) names things; UI copy and components use those exact terms.
5. **Mobile truth, desktop generosity** — the phone layout is the reference experience; desktop re-composes the same components with more room, it never forks the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and interactive states.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for every animation (carousel auto-rotation included).
- Touch-first targets (≥40px) on mobile; visible focus states for keyboard use on desktop.
- English UI for v1; copy kept simple for non-native readers.
